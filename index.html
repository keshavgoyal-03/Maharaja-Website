Our Story

A journey of passion since 1992

In the heart of Bulandshahr, on the bustling Ansari Road, a dream took shape in 1992. What began as a modest readymade garments store has today blossomed into Maharaja Readymade — a name synonymous with trust, quality, and royal fashion. For over three decades, we have dressed generations of families, witnessed countless weddings, celebrated festivals, and become a part of the fabric of this beautiful city.

From a single counter to 5 grand levels spanning 10,000 square feet, our journey reflects the aspirations of every customer who walked through our doors. Under the visionary leadership of Nitesh Goyal, supported by Rajeev Goyal and Ravinder Goyal, Maharaja Readymade continues to set new benchmarks in fashion retail across Western Uttar Pradesh.

1992
The Beginning
Maharaja Readymade opens its doors on Ansari Road, Bulandshahr, with a vision to bring quality readymade fashion to the city.
2000
First Expansion
Growing demand leads to expansion. The store now occupies multiple floors with dedicated sections for men, women, and children.
2010
Brand Partnerships
Partnerships with premium brands like U.S. Polo Assn., Van Heusen, Monte Carlo, and more transform Maharaja into a multi-brand destination.
2015
5 Levels Complete
The store reaches its current magnificent form — 5 grand levels with dedicated bridal, ethnic, formal, casual, and accessories sections.
2020
Digital Presence
Embracing the digital age with WhatsApp Business and Instagram, bringing the Maharaja experience online for customers everywhere.
Today
500,000+ Families
Serving over 0+ happy families and still continuing, Maharaja Readymade stands as the undisputed royal address of fashion in Bulandshahr.
